Materials & Construction

Chosen for heat, humidity, and dust.

FRAMEPowder-coated galvanised steel — welded, corrosion-proof in humid heat, locally fabricable.
DOORSteel + EPDM magnetic gasket (UV-stable, 18–24 mo) · 3× 304 stainless hinges · 12 V solenoid bolt.
FLOORAnti-slip aluminium tread plate — drains condensate, takes crate load.
REFRIGERANTR290 (propane), GWP 3 — Secop BD35F hermetic DC compressor.
HEAT EXCH.Roll-bond aluminium evaporator + finned condenser, externally vented, 12 V fan.
EXTERIORWhite PU topcoat — reflects solar load; black would absorb 20–30% more heat.

The Trust Layer · core IP

A record a lender can trust — by construction.

01

Device-authenticated

A per-pod X.509 certificate; a record from any unauthenticated source cannot enter the system.

02

Independently witnessed

Each transaction reconciles against the M-Pesa settlement API — a payment network no party controls.

03

Tamper-evident

Append-only, offline-first telemetry; gaps from lost connectivity are marked, never hidden.

The Credit Pipeline · in development

Behavioral data → a score a regulated lender can act on.

We use transactional, behavioral data — a stronger credit signal than yield prediction (Accion, 2026). Trained only after the pilot; scores stay advisory until a lender validates.
