About this demo

This is a working sample demo of FarmPilot, not the final product UI. Every tab is interactive — diagnose with your camera or a photo, chat with the advisor, check sell-timing, manage your crops and settings.

The diagnosis engine is real: a 6 MB on-device CNN at 97.8% validation accuracy on public crop-disease data. In this demo, camera/upload results use a lightweight on-image heuristic for illustration; the production model runs the full CNN on-device.
