About this demo

This is a working sample demo of FarmPilot, not the final product UI. Every tab is interactive — diagnose a leaf, chat with the advisor, check sell-timing, add your crops.

The diagnosis engine behind it is real: a 6 MB on-device CNN at 97.8% validation accuracy on public crop-disease data. Voice and price layers are in active development; sample data shown for illustration.
