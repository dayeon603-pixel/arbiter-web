Tollgate

Sanctions and Travel Rule decisions pinned to the list as it stood, replayable offline.

Runs read-only beside the screener a firm already uses, so adopting it needs no replacement and never becomes the block-of-record.

When an examiner asks why a counterparty was blocked six months ago, the usual answer is that the list has moved, the vendor’s system has been updated, and the decision can no longer be reproduced. A Tollgate receipt is re-derived from the inputs that produced it, in a browser, with no account and no call back to us.

Reproducibility is offered on the exact-match path only. That limit is stated on the verifier itself rather than buried, because a green check over a match the system cannot actually reproduce would defeat the point of the product.

How it works

A record that outlives the vendor

Three steps turn a screening decision into evidence someone else can check.

  1. 01

    Pin the decision

    The verdict is recorded against a hash-identified snapshot of the list as it stood at that moment, not against whatever the list says today.

  2. 02

    Seal it into a chain

    Each receipt is signed and carries the hash of the one before it. Deleting a record, editing one, or reordering two of them all break the chain.

  3. 03

    Let anyone re-derive it

    A third party recomputes the hash from the receipt and checks the signature, offline, with no account and no call back to us.
