How it works

A record that outlives the vendor

Three steps turn a screening decision into evidence someone else can check.

  1. 01

    Pin the decision

    The verdict is recorded against a hash-identified snapshot of the list as it stood at that moment, not against whatever the list says today.

  2. 02

    Seal it into a chain

    Each receipt is signed and carries the hash of the one before it. Deleting a record, editing one, or reordering two of them all break the chain.

  3. 03

    Let anyone re-derive it

    A third party recomputes the hash from the receipt and checks the signature, offline, with no account and no call back to us.
